# Warrant/OPM Alignment Policy

**Date:** 2026-05
**Category:** methodology

## Summary

For warrants with dual settlement options (cash payment or preferred shares), Carta decided to treat the warrants as economically equivalent to the underlying preferred shares in the OPM — not model a separate senior cash breakpoint — because modeling the cash breakpoint separately would double-count the economic claim and overstate the warrant's position relative to other preferred holders.

## Context

Firmaye Tarekegn raised an alignment issue in May 2026: Series B Preferred Warrants with a $0.01 exercise price had two settlement options — (1) Series B Preferred Stock, or (2) a cash payment per a Supplement Provision. The warrant valuation had modeled a senior cash breakpoint, but the 409A had treated the warrants as penny warrants (economically equivalent to preferred). Auditors asked why the two reports didn't align. The question was escalated to Jon Grupp for a sanity check before sending the response to auditors.

## What Was Decided

- Warrants with dual settlement options (cash or preferred) should be treated as economically equivalent to the underlying preferred in the OPM
- The two settlement forms represent equivalent economic claims on company value; the cash amount is structured to approximate the equity settlement value
- Modeling an explicit senior cash breakpoint would double-count this economic claim and overstate the warrant's position relative to other Series B holders
- The "best economic outcome" principle should guide treatment: an investor would choose whichever settlement maximizes value, so the OPM should reflect the preferred breakpoints
- Auditor response: explain economic equivalence and that the 409A correctly follows FMV principles under IRC 409A regulations

## Key Quotes

> "As participating shares I am guessing including them in the breakpoints for the 409A reduces the value to common and that is why audit is asking. Since the warrants have the option to take either the cash position or the Series B shares we should follow the best economic outcome for the warrants as that is what an investor and market participant would do." — Jon Grupp, May 2026

> "We treat the warrants as economically equivalent to Series B Preferred shares and allocate them through the OPM at the same liquidation breakpoint and payoff structure as the underlying Series B Preferred Stock... Modeling an explicit senior cash breakpoint would double-count this economic claim and overstate the warrant's position relative to other Series B holders." — Firmaye Tarekegn (auditor response draft), May 2026
