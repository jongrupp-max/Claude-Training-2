# IRA Transfer Custom Engagement

**Date:** 2026-06
**Category:** client-handling

## Summary

For clients requiring a signed valuation document for IRA share transfer purposes (where an IRA custodian requires the valuation provider's signature), Carta determined it would not sign the 409A itself but would explore a custom engagement — similar to a Gift & Estate valuation — where Legal would amend the engagement letter to fit the client's specific needs.

## Context

A prospect had 30 shareholders who transfer shares into an IRA. Their prior CPA had signed the 409A for the IRA custodian's form. When the client wanted to switch to Carta, they required a signed valuation from Carta. Ian Kawamoto had initially raised this with Jon Grupp in a DM. The situation resurfaced in #val-sales in June 2026 when Ariana Nurisso needed a definitive answer. The client had a hard requirement that could not be met by Carta's standard 409A product.

## What Was Decided

- Carta will not sign a 409A report under any circumstances — this is a firm policy
- For IRA transfer situations requiring a signed document from the valuation provider, a custom engagement can be explored — structured similarly to a G&E engagement
- Legal would need to amend the Engagement Letter to be specific to the client's IRA custodian requirements
- This is not a scalable product; it's a bespoke engagement requiring coordination with Legal
- The question of whether a G&E report can serve the IRA purpose (replacing the CPA-signed form) requires legal review
