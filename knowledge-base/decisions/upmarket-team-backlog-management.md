# Upmarket Team Backlog Management

**Date:** 2026-06
**Category:** operational

## Summary

An "Upmarket team" was established to handle the high end of 409A delivery, with a dedicated backlog sheet for assignment. Evan Rydinski identified a gap in senior coverage at this tier and sought commitments from Jon Grupp and Rizwan Ahmed on how much they could cover without analyst support.

## Context

In April 2026, Evan Rydinski flagged in a group DM with Jon Grupp, Ian Kawamoto, and Rizwan Ahmed that there was insufficient coverage at the high end of 409A delivery. By June 2026, the Upmarket team was operational with its own backlog sheet and assignment workflow. Automated alerts in #erp-delivery-alerts began tagging the Upmarket team for prioritization of specific deliveries.

## What Was Decided

- An Upmarket team handles complex/high-value 409A deliveries separately from the standard delivery queue
- A dedicated backlog sheet tracks Upmarket assignments with a separate tab
- Automated delivery alerts route Upmarket projects to the appropriate team member (Evan Rydinski or Nick Garrett based on assignment)
- Senior advisors (Jon Grupp, Rizwan Ahmed) committed to handling a share of complex deliveries without requiring analyst support underneath them

## People Involved

- **[Evan Rydinski](../people/evan-rydinski.md)** — Head of Valuation Delivery; designed and implemented the Upmarket team structure

## Key Quotes

> "Yeah, to clarify, I'm looking for a commitment on what you both can do without analyst help. The issue is not having enough coverage on the high end of 409A delivery, so that is the gap I'm looking to fill." — Evan Rydinski, Apr 2026
