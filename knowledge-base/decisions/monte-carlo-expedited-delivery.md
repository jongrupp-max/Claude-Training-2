# Monte Carlo Expedited Delivery Precedent

**Date:** 2026-06
**Category:** operational

## Summary

After a delivery miss where performance units were not modeled consistent with a prior provider's approach, Jennifer Hamblet and Jon Grupp performed a complex Monte Carlo analysis and breakpoint sensitivity under urgent audit pressure, establishing a precedent for expedited complex deliveries when Carta has made a delivery error.

## Context

A customer's 409A was delivered without correctly modeling performance units in the way their prior provider had. The customer had an active audit and demanded a Monte Carlo revision by end of day. Evan Rydinski documented the situation in #valuations-csat in June 2026 after the case was escalated. The team performed the complex work expeditiously despite the unrealistic timeline, but the combination of the initial delivery miss and the inability to fully meet the EOD expectation remained a sticking point for the customer.

## What Was Decided

- When Carta has made a delivery error, the response is expedited complex work regardless of timeline difficulty
- Monte Carlo and breakpoint sensitivity can be performed on short turnaround when the error is on Carta's side
- Unrealistic customer timelines (EOD same day for Monte Carlo) cannot always be met, but the team should make a best-faith effort
- The delivery miss is a learning opportunity; the post-delivery response was handled correctly
