# Foreign Client 409A Usage Guidance

**Date:** 2025-07
**Category:** methodology

## Summary

Carta established guidance that foreign clients (Canada, Netherlands, India, etc.) may use Carta's 409A reports as an indication of FMV for local option issuance, but they bear the risk of non-specific guidance and must confirm applicability with their own legal and tax advisors.

## Context

Field teams were repeatedly encountering clients with non-US employees asking whether a 409A is sufficient for local regulatory purposes. Jackson Reed raised the question in #valuators in July 2025 after two same-day engagements with Netherlands and Canadian companies. There is no formal equivalent to IRC 409A in most countries, but a defensible, reasonable FMV determination is generally required.

## What Was Decided

- Carta will not provide country-specific tax guidance — support is scoped to 409A matters only
- Foreign clients may use the 409A report, but bear the "risk" of applying it outside its intended regulatory scope
- Clients should be directed to confirm with local legal/tax counsel
- In practice, many foreign clients (especially Canadian companies) use the 409A without issue; this is not grounds to refuse service
- India is a notable exception: some Indian clients have been told their local regulators won't accept a 409A and may require an independent local report
