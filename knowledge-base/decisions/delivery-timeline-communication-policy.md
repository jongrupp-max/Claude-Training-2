# Delivery Timeline Communication Policy

**Date:** 2026-06
**Category:** operational

## Summary

Carta codified that nearly all 409A customers receive a delivery timeline estimate once their request has been processed, with the timeline varying by segment. Two exceptions exist: companies on the LLC platform and customers requesting non-409A valuations (Gift Tax, Warrant Val, etc.).

## Context

In June 2026, a CSM (João Pedro Gravino) asked in #valuation-support about setting delivery timeline exceptions for specific customers. Evan Rydinski clarified that a systematic delivery timeline process was already in place and CSMs did not need to manage exceptions manually. This formalized what had been a more ad-hoc practice and made clear that the delivery team owns timeline communication.

## What Was Decided

- 409A customers receive a delivery timeline estimate after their request is processed — this is standard, not exception-based
- Timeline varies by segment (e.g. pre-seed/benchmarking vs. complex/upmarket)
- LLC platform customers do not receive a delivery timeline estimate
- Non-409A valuations (Gift Tax, Warrant Valuation, etc.) do not receive a delivery timeline estimate
- CSMs should not attempt to set exceptions — the delivery team has a system for this

## People Involved

- **[Evan Rydinski](../people/evan-rydinski.md)** — Head of Valuation Delivery; established and communicated the policy
