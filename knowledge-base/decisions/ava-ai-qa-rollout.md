# AVA AI QA System Rollout

**Date:** 2025-08
**Category:** methodology

## Summary

Carta decided to move 80%+ of 409A Quality Assurance to an AI tool called AVA (built on Google Gemini Gems), rolling out in phases starting with DLOM, then Comparable Companies, Pre-Seed Benchmarking, and eventually all Standard Non-Benchmarking 409A workflows.

## Context

The valuation team was performing QA manually across every 409A. Kristoffer Warren and Brendan Rigby led an initiative to codify QA policies in an AI-friendly format so that Gemini could execute checks automatically. The goal was to reduce analyst burden, improve consistency, and free up capacity for higher-complexity work. The rollout started in August 2025 and expanded significantly by September 2025 when AVA covered all Standard Non-Benchmarking 409A.

## What Was Decided

- AVA would be used for QA on all applicable 409A workflows, with mandatory adoption once a block was released
- Analysts failing to get correct results should file a Google Form so the policy could be improved
- Expansion roadmap: DTC, DTC EA, Basket Backsolve, DCF, Non-Revenue Multiple GPCs planned as next phases
- An agentic workflow would eventually replace the manual Gemini Gem approach
