# Monte Carlo Audit Objection Response

**Date:** 2026-06
**Category:** client-handling

## Summary

Carta established a standard rebuttal to the auditor objection that "Carta historically cannot use Monte Carlo models." The official position is that Carta can deploy Monte Carlo for path-dependent or complex capital structures when determined to be the most appropriate methodology, and that Carta evaluates the appropriateness of all methods for each engagement.

## Context

In June 2026, a PE-backed LLC prospect evaluating Carta for cap table + 409A services shared an auditor concern that Carta's methodology might not pass audit due to inability to use Monte Carlo. Ashley Ryan posted the concern in #val-sales. This was a recurring sales objection. Rizwan Ahmed drafted a comprehensive rebuttal that was shared with the channel and became the standard response for sales teams to deploy.

## What Was Decided

- Carta's official position: the team evaluates OPM Back-Solve, Income Approach (DCF), and Market Approaches for every valuation and selects based on company stage, financing recency, and forecast reliability
- Carta can handle complex capital structures including seniority tranches, liquidation preferences, participating rights, and performance-based vesting
- Carta can deploy Monte Carlo simulations for path-dependent capital structures when that methodology is determined to be most appropriate — contrary to the auditor's assertion
- This response is standardized for sales and CSM use when the Monte Carlo objection arises

## Key Quotes

> "Methodology Selection & Weighting: For every valuation, Carta evaluates the appropriateness of the OPM Back-Solve, Income Approach (DCF), and Market Approaches (Guideline Public/Transactions). Our final selection and the relative weightings assigned to each method are driven by the company's stage of development, the recency/relevance of arm's-length financing rounds, and the reliability of available financial forecasts." — Rizwan Ahmed, Jun 2026

> "Advanced Modeling (Monte Carlo & Path-Dependent Features): While standard 409A valuations typically leverage the closed-form Black-Scholes OPM, Carta can handle complex valuations also as needed. If a company's capital structure features path-dependent elements, we do have the capability to deploy Monte Carlo simulations to isolate value." — Rizwan Ahmed, Jun 2026
