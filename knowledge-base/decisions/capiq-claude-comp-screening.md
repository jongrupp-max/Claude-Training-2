# CapIQ + Claude AI Comp Screening Integration

**Date:** 2026-04
**Category:** operational

## Summary

CapIQ data was integrated into Carta's internal Claude AI marketplace, with initial skills released for 409A comparable company screening — giving analysts a structured AI-assisted workflow for identifying and evaluating public comps.

## Context

Comparable company selection is one of the more time-intensive and judgment-heavy steps in a 409A. Jordan Agnew announced in #valuators in April 2026 that CapIQ had been made available through Carta's internal Claude marketplace alongside the first 409A comp screening skills. This built on the broader AVA initiative (see [AVA AI QA System Rollout](ava-ai-qa-rollout.md)) and extended AI assistance into the data-gathering phase of comp selection, not just QA.

## What Was Decided

- CapIQ is now accessible via Carta's internal Claude AI marketplace for analysts
- Initial skills cover 409A comp screening — enabling AI-assisted identification and evaluation of guideline public companies
- Intended to reduce the time analysts spend on manual CapIQ searches and improve consistency in comp selection
