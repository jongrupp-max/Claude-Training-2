# Advisor Delivery Model Restructure

**Date:** 2026-06
**Category:** operational

## Summary

Carta transitioned 409A delivery responsibility to a new Advisor function, replacing the prior queue system with a consolidated hub called "Corporation Station" and an Advisor Assignment Queue. Senior methodology team members (Brendan Rigby, Daniel Gladstone, Jon Grupp, Rizwan Ahmed) were asked to help clear the backlog as Advisors ramped up.

## Context

As of June 2026, Evan Rydinski communicated that the team was transitioning to a new operational model where Advisors handle the delivery function for 409As. The Advisors were still getting up to speed on delivery workflows, creating a backlog of projects due in short order. To prevent the Advisors from being overwhelmed at the start, the senior team was asked to take on projects from the unassigned 409A section of the queue. The new "Corporation Station" tracker replaced prior queue systems and provided a consolidated delivery and metrics hub.

## What Was Decided

- Advisors take primary responsibility for 409A delivery as their core function
- "Corporation Station" is the new consolidated delivery and metrics hub; assignments tracked via dropdown in the row
- Senior team members pick up backlog projects during the transition ramp period
- New model separates the delivery function from the advisory/methodology function more cleanly
