# Biotech Hybrid Scenario Weighting Adjustment

**Date:** 2026-02
**Category:** methodology

## Summary

For biotech clients who fundamentally disagree with the backsolve methodology and push for a lower Probability of IPO (PoP), Carta established precedent for adjusting Hybrid scenario weightings (e.g. 90/10 late/early exit weighting at 8% PoP) as an accommodation — particularly when the frustration stems from using a non-biotech-specific model.

## Context

A first-time 409A client (biotech company) disagreed with Carta's backsolve methodology. The analyst had arrived at 14% PoP; the client was pushing for 10%. Brendan Rigby escalated to Daniel Gladstone in #advisory-services-questions in February 2026 and noted there was existing precedent for adjusting Hybrid scenario weightings for biotech companies, framing it as an aggressive draft option at 8% PoP with 90/10 weighting.

## What Was Decided

- Carta has precedent for adjusting Hybrid scenario weightings for biotech companies when there is a legitimate methodological basis
- An aggressive draft (e.g. 90/10 weighting at 8% PoP) can be prepared to illustrate sensitivity and address client concern
- The root cause of disagreement — that the model is not biotech-specific — is a valid context for accommodation
- Decision requires sign-off from a senior methodology reviewer (Brendan Rigby / Daniel Gladstone level)
